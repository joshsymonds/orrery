What differs today, worst first

The built page is the E27b build on :8494, looked at with the owner's real sessions on it, so it is not reproduced here. W4 marks what the reader rework in progress already answers, read from its source at effort/orrery-web-reader-markdown-1, not from a render. The full list, with the proposal for each, is in inbox-gap.md.

No frame. The page is a document 1200 px wide that scrolls as a whole, one colour throughout. View B is three surfaces that fill the window and scroll apart. W4 half The columns now fill and scroll; the surfaces, the fixed widths and the header that spans all three remain.

The reader has no head and no foot. Nothing says which conversation is open, its state or its timer; the composer and the working line float at the end of the text. open

Tool output floods the reader. One result can fill the window in monospace. View B folds a run of calls to a line. W4 Each call is now one line with its result folded; runs are not grouped yet.

Epics crowd out the sessions. Every live epic is a full row, 25 on the day I looked, most of them finished. On a phone the sessions start three screens down. open

An epic row says almost nothing. A gauge and a count. View B has the stage, a track, each running step with five pips and what it is doing, and a tail. open

A step's conversation looks like the owner wrote the engine's half. The brief and every failed-check message are YOU bubbles. View B has a Brief fold and a card per round with a chip per check. open

The epic column has no hierarchy. Three plain lines, a small chart, a flat task list, a flat step list. View B has a head, the chart with its count, units with pips and a status line, the cursor's unit ringed, and a card for what is running. open

Rows lost their detail. Glyphs are bare discs with no ?, ✓ or !, and the working one does not turn; every row leads with its state word; the second line is cut to one; section heads have no count and no colour. open

No counts, no mark. "5 need you, 3 working, 3 new" is the page's answer at a glance. The built page has the word orrery. open

The answer keys are blind. 1, 2 and 3 answer a question from the list, but the row does not show what they are. View B prints them as chips. open

The clock overruns its gutter ("03:26 PM" in five characters) and the text has no measure: at 1600 a line runs past 100 characters. View B prints 14:19 and stops at 68. open

The key list lives at the bottom of the list column, under everything. View B keeps keys where the action is and the list behind ?. open

Where the proposal beats view B, and where it cannot match it

Beats it

  • One drawing of progress, three sizes. The five pips on a step row, the strip under an epic and the chart in the column are the same cells from the same slices. In view B the pips, the stage track and the rings are three different measures.
  • The task strip under each epic is real: a segment per task in its state. View B's five-stage track needs a stage that orrery does not record.
  • The chart answers back. Its centre reads the cursor's task and any slice you point at.
  • Check rounds come from the step's stored checks, one chip each, so a failed round shows which check failed without opening anything.
  • The epic column is 380 wide, so the chart holds 35 tasks at a readable size; view B's 320 fits its 7.

Cannot match it yet: no data behind it

  • The stage track (contract, build, review, release, report).
  • Requirements and their evidence dots.
  • Outlined units and the "circle grew" note.
  • The model beside each unit, and the monitor's verdict and model time.
  • Decision, contract and release rows in Needs you. The web list has panes and sessions; a held step shows under its epic.
  • Host load in the footer, the context meter in the composer, ⌘K.

And one thing it gives up on purpose: view B sets prose in Atkinson Hyperlegible. The owner's later call is Orrery Mono everywhere, so the proposal's text is mono at 14/1.6 and its rows are set a half point smaller to hold view B's line lengths.
