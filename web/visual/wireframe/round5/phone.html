Motion note the web view's tokens, as Compose

The durations and curves are the web view's: 120 ms for press, 200 ms for a state change, 280 ms for layout, 320 ms for a page change, 200 ms for anything leaving. FastOutSlowInEasing is the nearest stock curve for entering and standard moves; CubicBezierEasing(0.2f, 0f, 0f, 1f) is the exact one. FastOutLinearInEasing is the leaving curve exactly. With animations off in system settings, placement snaps and a change is at most a 100 ms fade.

  • A state phrase changes200 ms
    Crossfade(targetState = phrase, animationSpec = tween(200, easing = FastOutSlowInEasing))
  • A unit finishes200 ms, then 320 ms
    AnimatedContent on the mark, spinner to check, with fadeIn(tween(200)) + scaleIn(initialScale = 0.8f). animateColorAsState(tween(320)) cools the check and clears the wash.
  • An epic ends280 ms · 200 ms
    The lazy list's items are keyed by epic id and carry Modifier.animateItem(placementSpec = tween(280, easing = FastOutSlowInEasing)), so the row glides to Just ended. The open page fills in its Report with AnimatedVisibility.
  • A task moves on the orrery200 ms
    Each slice's colour is an animateColorAsState(tween(200)), so the slice it left cools to a tick as the slice it reached lights. The glow on a slice in progress is an infiniteRepeatable alpha on a blurred stroke, still when animations are off.
  • The live sentence updates200 ms
    Crossfade on the sentence. The “updated” marker restarts at “just now” and washes once with animateColorAsState(tween(320)).
  • One page deeper, one page back320 ms in, 200 ms out
    The NavHost's transitions: slideInHorizontally(tween(320, easing = FastOutSlowInEasing)) { it / 8 } + fadeIn() together with slideOutHorizontally(tween(200, easing = FastOutLinearInEasing)) { -it / 12 } + fadeOut(). Back mirrors it, and the page returned to keeps its scroll position.
  • An attention item arrives280 ms · 200 ms
    animateItem moves the rows below. The new row enters with fadeIn() + expandVertically(). The count is an AnimatedContent.
  • The send box's edge200 ms
    animateColorAsState(tween(200)) between the working, waiting and idle colours.

The dashed tags mark what orrery does not send today. They are off until the bar's switch turns them on. The full list is in the web view's notes and at the end of the flows.

Decided for the phone Nov 11 2026 · all eight accepted · five changed Nov 14

  1. Epics get their own group

    Epics sit between Needs you and Sessions, on the phone and in the web inbox. A step never appears among sessions; it is reached through its epic.

  2. Home shows only an epic's running or paused steps

    Changed Nov 14. On Home an epic is a line with its orrery. Its units in flight are on its page, under the orrery, each with how long it has been where it is.

  3. Four kinds count as “Needs you”

    Changed Nov 14. A needs-owner decision, a session's question, a contract ready to accept, and a release step that failed. Each says what is waiting and why. Decisions and questions notify at once; contracts and a failed release step notify quietly. Steps stay silent, as O1a's contract says.

  4. A message to a step is a nudge, and says so

    The send box's placeholder says “Nudge export-cli”. Interrupt is its own button. Hold, Resume and Stop are buttons in the title row of the step's phase; Stop asks first.

  5. A decision is picked, then confirmed

    Changed Nov 14. The form is the first block of the epic view. It shows the line it will write to decisions.md before the confirm button. A session's question keeps its one tap.

  6. A contract can be accepted from the phone

    At limited and serious care, once no decision or added row is open. At severe care the folded “can't be undone” section must also have been opened.

  7. Release runs itself

    Changed Nov 14, after O3e3. orrery runs a clean epic's release steps and asks you only when a step fails, or when a step that cannot be undone would have to be repeated. Retry and Stop are the first block of the epic view.

  8. The engine's own steps are one-line rows

    Changed Nov 14. Planner turns, reviewers and retries are one line each with their end kind in words, coloured only when bad. They open in place under the units in flight, and they are in the history.
