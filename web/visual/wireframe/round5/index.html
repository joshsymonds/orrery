Warp and herdr

Both keep agents inside a terminal and add a status per pane: working, blocked or waiting, idle. Warp's orchestration view draws the parent with one pill per child agent.

Three states is the right amount, and orrery has them. A parent with children is the epic view; rings carry more than pills.
