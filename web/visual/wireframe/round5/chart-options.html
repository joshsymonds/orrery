Chosen: 1 · Orbits, with the owner's three changes

1. No dot and no halo. The phase a task is working toward is a cell, like every other phase.

2. Colour by phase, per cell. Green: that phase succeeded; if something succeeded it is green, on a running task as on a finished one. Yellow: the phase a running task is working toward, the first ring it has not yet reached counting from red test inward, and the only yellow in the chart. A task that has just started is yellow on red test. Peach: that same ring on a held task, still. Red: the phase that errored, on a task that ended without done: checks when the gate kept failing, gate accepted when the judge said unmet, otherwise the first ring it had not reached. The track tone: not reached.

3. The glow is on the cell, not the slice. The yellow cell stays at full strength and a blurred copy of it breathes over it, spilling a little on its neighbours. The whole slice lit is drawn beside it for comparison: it finds the task as well, but it lays yellow over the green cells the task has passed, and green must mean passed. At 6 tasks it is a slab a sixth of the chart wide. Under reduced motion the glow holds still at half strength.
