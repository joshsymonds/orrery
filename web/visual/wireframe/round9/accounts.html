round 9 · first drawing

Accounts: groups, directories, and what they cost

Work accounts apart from personal ones, in groups you name and colour; the account a conversation uses, its load and its cost in a band of the conversation's head, beside the machine's; a default for orrery and a pin for any directory prefix, permanent until changed; and no list price anywhere. It extends round 8's LLM usage drawing, which stays the reference for everything not named here: the collapsed rail's tile is unchanged and shows the default account. Inter sets reading text; Maple Mono sets labels, paths, ids and figures. Every section sits on one 1192 px grid. All accounts, directories, hosts and amounts are invented. What no accepted epic sends is marked needs the server.

1 · the inbox

The usage band, by group

Round 8's table, its columns and their heads unchanged. Accounts now sort by group, in the groups' order, each run under a thin divider naming its group in its colour; the ungrouped come last under no group. Each account's dot, or the limited glyph in its place, takes its group's colour. A billed account (an API key, a Bedrock or OpenRouter seat) has no limit, so it shows what it billed in the last day, 7 days and 31 days in the three window columns, each window named in its bar's place. The default account's name is bold. The band stays on by default; a click opens the popover (2).

  • newGroup dividers; dots and marks in the group's colour; billed rows with 1d, 7d and 31d; the default in bold.
  • newA project card in the inbox names the account its directory uses, under its host.
  • goneThe Billed 7d … list line, and the filled mauve dot of the one account in use.
2 · the popover

Every account in full: Make default, and Use for a directory

The band's table at reading size, divided by group the same way. Under each name: who uses it (its pins, or every unpinned directory for the default), then its plan and when it was read, or for a billed account its kind. A billed account's cells are its spend with the window under it. Make default sets the account every directory without a pin falls back to; it asks in its row first, since running sessions move on their next request. Use… pins a directory to the account from a menu of the directories orrery knows, the same pin as the head's flow (5). The default's row carries the default chip in Make default's place.

  • newGroup dividers; billed rows; Make default; Use… for a directory; who uses each account by its pins.
  • goneThe billed, last 7 days foot and its list price; round 8's Use, which moved every context: with pins, that move is Make default.
3 · accounts and routes

Groups, accounts, directories, contexts, seats

The page gains two sections. Groups first, since they order everything under them: a group is created, renamed in place, recoloured from a fixed palette of six, dragged into order, or deleted, which leaves its accounts ungrouped. Accounts sort by group under the same dividers as the band, each with a group select and Make default. Directories lists every pin, and the default as the last row, with an example of which pin wins. Every refusal says what it refused and that nothing changed.

4 · the conversation head

The account band

A band under the machine band, styled as its sibling: on surface.side, an edge under it, its first column as wide as the machine band's so the host's meters and the account's start on one line. It reads: the account, by its mark, name and group; that account's load (5h, 7d and Fable bars for a subscription, 1d, 7d and 31d spend for a billed one); why the conversation has it, default, or a pin glyph and the prefix it is pinned by (its tip says pinned by ~/Work in words, which the band has no room for beside a long path); then, at its end, where the context sat in the title row, this conversation's own figures: the context and its usage, tokens on a subscription and billed dollars on a billed account. Usage opens the Usage panel (6). A press on the account opens Use another account for this directory (5). Hide the meters (m) folds both bands; the title row then keeps the account and the context in compact form. Heads are drawn at 1036, a 1440 window with the inbox open; under 960, as beside the Usage panel (6), both bands drop their bars and keep every figure.

  • newThe account band; the compact account and context in a folded title row.
  • goneThe context and the usage readout in the title row, open.
5 · the pin flow

Use another account for this directory

From the account in the head: every account, by group, each with the load that matters for choosing (5h and 7d, or what it billed in 7 days); then the prefix the pin goes on, offered as this conversation's directory and each parent up to ~, each saying what the pin moves. A pin is permanent until changed. Pin asks in the foot, naming what moves and what stays. Where the directory already has its own pin, the foot offers to remove it, falling back to the next pin or the default.

  • newAll of it: the popover, its asking foot, refusal, the done toast, and Remove the pin.
6 · usage

What a conversation, and an epic, used

The Usage panel opens from the account band's usage. Subscription traffic is counted in tokens, with nothing billed; billed traffic in dollars, with its tokens beside them. The panel names the account it ran on. An epic's Usage section names the accounts its sessions ran on, then each session with its account, tokens, and what it billed where it billed. The bar is each session's share of the epic's tokens. Needs the server: a session's account, and the epic's rows summed by session.

  • newTokens for subscription traffic; the account a conversation and each epic session ran on.
  • goneList price: the panel's at list price, its dollars per kind and per model on a subscription, the epic's list column and total.
7 · phone

Android and the web under 760

Phones are drawn at the column's width, 381 dp. The band is the desk's, groups and all; a tap opens the popover as a sheet. In a conversation the machine band stays hidden, as today, and the account band takes three lines: the account and why; its 5h, 7d and Fable; then this conversation's context and usage. Folded, the title row keeps the account's mark and the context. The pin flow is a sheet, and Pin asks in a sheet of its own. Groups are edited in a sheet, with Move up and Move down for order.
