cB2c’s flyout, and what a click does
The flyout

It opens beside the tile on hover or with p, and holds the project’s name and path, its counts, and its rows that need you: those that wait first, with what they ask in peach, then the new ones. Everything else is one line of counts. Its foot opens the project’s page.

A click on a tile

Goes to the flyout’s first row: the first row that waits on you, or with none the first new row, or with neither the project’s page.

Why: hover already shows the flyout, so a click that only opened it would add nothing. Expanding the inbox to the project undoes the collapse you chose. Going to the first row makes the rail a one-click triage control, the same move as w scoped to one project, and the flyout under the pointer has already told you which row it will be.
